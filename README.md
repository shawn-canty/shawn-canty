#### [Telco Customer Churn & Retention Strategy](https://github.com/shawn-canty/telco-churn-analysis)
* **Tech Stack:** Advanced Excel (Formulas, Pivot Tables) · Behavioral Segmentation · Risk Modeling
* **Scope & Method:** Evaluated customer lifecycle patterns and service add-ons across 7,043 accounts to isolate structural drivers of subscriber attrition.
* **Key Finding:** Uncovered a 50.4% cancellation rate for Month-to-Month contracts lacking tech support, compounding to 58.4% when paired with electronic check billing.
* **Business Impact:** Recommended mandatory onboarding incentives for tech support and multi-year contract discounting, projected to reduce first-year churn by 15–20%.
* **Deliverables:** [Executive Slide Deck (PDF)]([https://github.com/shawn-canty/telco-churn-analysis](https://github.com/shawn-canty/telco-churn-analysis/blob/main/telco_executive_slide_deck.pdf)) · [Interactive Excel Dashboard (.xlsx)](https://github.com/shawn-canty/telco-churn-analysis)

#### [Algorithmic Model Audit: Predictive Stability & Error Calibration](https://github.com/shawn-canty/nba-predictive-model-audit)
* **Tech Stack:** Google BigQuery (SQL) · Tableau · Statistical Modeling · Hypothesis Testing
* **Scope & Method:** Backtested a quantitative projection engine across 397 market-clearing consensus events to evaluate baseline accuracy, variance, and systemic bias.
* **Key Finding:** Isolated positive predictive alpha in Rebound metrics with a 55.15% accuracy rate exceeding the 52.4% transaction break-even benchmark, while uncovering a structural directional over-indexing bias (64–75%) on high-variance metrics.
* **Business Impact:** Instituted strict validation gates (MAE error tolerances and sample stability thresholds), advising against production deployment until variance filters are calibrated.
* **Deliverables:** [Live Tableau Dashboard](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/shawn.canty/vizzes) · [Executive Slide Deck (PDF)](https://github.com/shawn-canty/nba-predictive-model-audit) · [BigQuery SQL Scripts](https://github.com/shawn-canty/nba-predictive-model-audit)

#### [Cyclistic Bike-Share Behavioral Analysis](https://github.com/shawn-canty/cyclistic-bike-share-analysis)
* **Tech Stack:** Google BigQuery (SQL) · Tableau · Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
* **Scope & Method:** Consolidated, cleaned, and analyzed 5.9M+ historical trip records to map behavioral patterns between Casual users and Annual Members.
* **Key Finding:** Casual riders averaged 19+ minutes per ride primarily on weekends near leisure/waterfront locations, contrasting with commuter members who peaked during weekday transit windows.
* **Business Impact:** Proposed seasonal weekend membership tiers targeted at high-traffic leisure stations to convert recreational volume into recurring subscription revenue.
* **Deliverables:** [Interactive Tableau Dashboard](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/shawn.canty/vizzes) · [Executive Presentation (PDF)](https://github.com/shawn-canty/cyclistic-bike-share-analysis) · [BigQuery SQL ETL Scripts](https://github.com/shawn-canty/cyclistic-bike-share-analysis)

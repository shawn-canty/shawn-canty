## 🚀 Featured Case Studies

### [NBA Predictive Model & Heuristic Calibration](https://github.com/shawn-canty/nba-predictive-model-audit)
* **Tech Stack:** Google BigQuery (SQL) · Tableau · Statistical Modeling · Hypothesis Testing
* **Scope & Method:** Built a quantitative forecasting model to evaluate profitability across 397 active bets, testing performance against the standard sportsbook break-even threshold (-110 odds / 52.38% win rate).
* **Key Finding:** Achieved a profitable 55.15% overall win rate on Rebound markets, while identifying that High-Volatility models (64–75% "More" picks) suffered from directional bias and failed secondary stability gates.
* **Deliverables:** [Interactive Tableau Dashboard](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/shawn.canty) · [Executive Presentation (PDF)](https://github.com/shawn-canty/nba-predictive-model-audit) · [BigQuery SQL ETL Scripts](https://github.com/shawn-canty/nba-predictive-model-audit)

### [Cyclistic Bike-Share Behavioral Analysis](https://github.com/shawn-canty/cyclistic-bike-share-analysis)
* **Tech Stack:** Google BigQuery (SQL) · Tableau · Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
* **Scope & Method:** Consolidated, cleaned, and analyzed 5.9M+ historical trip records to map behavioral patterns between Casual users and Annual Members.
* **Key Finding:** Casual riders averaged 19+ minutes per ride primarily on weekends near leisure/waterfront locations, contrasting with commuter members who peaked during weekday transit windows.
* **Deliverables:** [Interactive Tableau Dashboard](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/shawn.canty) · [Executive Presentation (PDF)](https://github.com/shawn-canty/cyclistic-bike-share-analysis) · [BigQuery SQL ETL Scripts](https://github.com/shawn-canty/cyclistic-bike-share-analysis)

### [Telco Customer Churn & Retention Strategy](https://github.com/shawn-canty/telco-churn-analysis)
* **Tech Stack:** Advanced Excel (Formulas, Pivot Tables) · Behavioral Segmentation · Risk Modeling
* **Scope & Method:** Evaluated customer lifecycle patterns and service add-ons across 7,043 accounts to isolate structural drivers of subscriber attrition.
* **Key Finding:** Uncovered a 50.4% cancellation rate for Month-to-Month contracts lacking tech support, compounding to 58.4% when paired with electronic check billing.
* **Deliverables:** [Executive Slide Deck (PDF)](https://github.com/shawn-canty/telco-churn-analysis) · [Interactive Excel Dashboard (.xlsx)](https://github.com/shawn-canty/telco-churn-analysis)

### 🚀 Featured Case Studies

### [NBA Player Performance Predictive Model](https://github.com/shawn-canty/nba-predictive-model-audit)
* **Tech Stack:** Google BigQuery (SQL) · Tableau · Statistical Modeling
* **Scope & Method:** Retrograde Proof of Concept (PoC) auditing player props across 397 bets, evaluating sportsbook break-even thresholds and directional bias.
* **Key Finding:** Isolated an edge in Rebound props with a 55.15% win rate exceeding break-even targets, while identifying systemic over-indexing on "More" calls.
* **Deliverables:** [Live Tableau Dashboard](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/shawn.canty/viz/nba_predictive_model_analysis/Dashboard1) · [Executive Slide Deck (PDF)](https://github.com/shawn-canty/nba-predictive-model-audit/blob/main/nba_executive_presentation.pdf) · [BigQuery SQL Scripts](https://github.com/shawn-canty/nba-predictive-model-audit/blob/main/nba_model_poc_queries.sql)

#### [Telco Customer Churn & Retention Strategy](https://github.com/shawn-canty/telco-churn-analysis)
* **Tech Stack:** Advanced Excel (Formulas, Pivot Tables) · Behavioral Segmentation · Risk Modeling
* **Scope & Method:** Evaluated customer lifecycle patterns and service add-ons across 7,043 accounts to isolate drivers of customer churn.
* **Key Finding:** Uncovered a 50.4% cancellation rate for Month-to-Month contracts lacking tech support, compounding to 58.4% when paired with electronic check billing.
* **Deliverables:** [Executive Slide Deck (PDF)](https://github.com/shawn-canty/telco-churn-analysis/blob/main/telco_executive_slide_deck.pdf) · [Interactive Excel Dashboard (.xlsx)](https://github.com/shawn-canty/telco-churn-analysis/blob/main/telco_churn_dashboard_interactive.xlsx)

#### [Cyclistic Bike-Share Behavioral Analysis](https://github.com/shawn-canty/cyclistic-bike-share-analysis)
* **Tech Stack:** Google BigQuery (SQL) · Tableau · Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
* **Scope & Method:** Consolidated, cleaned, and analyzed 5.9M+ historical trip records to map behavioral differences between Casual riders and Annual Members.
* **Key Finding:** Casual riders averaged 19+ minutes per ride primarily on weekends at coastal landmarks, contrasting with commuter members who peaked mid-week.
* **Deliverables:** [Interactive Tableau Dashboard](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/shawn.canty/viz/Cyclistic_Rider_Analysis/Cyclistic_Rider_Analysis) · [Executive Presentation (PDF)](https://github.com/shawn-canty/cyclistic-bike-share-analysis/blob/main/Cyclistic_Executive_Presentation.pdf) · [BigQuery SQL ETL Scripts](https://github.com/shawn-canty/cyclistic-bike-share-analysis/blob/main/cyclistic_queries.sql)
